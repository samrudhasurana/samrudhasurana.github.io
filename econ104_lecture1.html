The share of newborns who die before age five

Outcome #2 — Maternal mortality

The share of mothers who die from pregnancy and childbirth

In the world's healthiest countries around 1800, roughly 900 mothers died for every 100,000 births — nearly 1 in 100. Today the high-income average is about 11 per 100,000.

Working definition

What is macroeconomics?

Macroeconomics

The study of the economy as a whole — explaining the economic changes that affect many households, firms, and markets simultaneously. Macroeconomists address diverse questions:

Why is average income high in some countries and low in others?
Why do prices sometimes rise rapidly, and at other times stay stable?
Why do production and employment expand in some years and contract in others?
What, if anything, can government policy do about growth, inflation, and employment?

These questions are all macroeconomic in nature because they concern the workings of the entire economy.
